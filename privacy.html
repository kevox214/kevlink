Privacy Policy

ⓘDraft placeholder for development. It is not a complete privacy notice. Obtain a data-protection review before collecting real personal data or launching in any jurisdiction.

Information used for account testing

When the local authentication backend is configured, it stores a submitted name, username, email address, phone number, password hash, an automatically generated personal referral code, referral attribution when a valid invite link is used, terms acceptance record, and short-lived hashed session token. Passwords are not stored in plain text.

Purpose and security

These details support account creation and sign-in testing. Use test information only. This development build does not provide identity verification, account recovery, or deletion workflows.

Production requirements

Before launch, publish the data controller's identity and contact details, legal basis, retention schedule, data subject rights, processors and transfers, breach procedures, and a documented deletion and access-request process. Collect only what is necessary.
